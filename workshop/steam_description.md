# EW Compats

**EW Compats** (EWC) connects other mods to Crow's Electronic Warfare, so their jammers and jamming effects work with its radio and spectrum systems.

## Requirements

- [CBA_A3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=450814997)
- [Crow's Electronic Warfare](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2515887728) (not needed for the Boomerang of CUP and RHS USAF and the Acoustic Drone Detection)

Every feature below is skipped when the mod it extends is not loaded.

## Features

- **BFT Jamming** (needs [cTab](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3438246217)) — editor module that turns an object into a jammer for the Blue Force Tracker: blackout, position drift or both, detectable with the Spectrum Device
- **Wi-Fi Jamming** (needs [Advanced Equipment](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2888888564)) — editor module that turns an object into a jammer that cuts the wireless connections of AE3 laptops to AE3 routers, with an effective and a falloff radius, detectable with the Spectrum Device
- **Acoustic Drone Detection** (no other mod needed) — editor or Zeus module that turns any object into an acoustic sensor. It hears drones from their sound (distance, terrain, weather and noise count), several sensors triangulate them, and the drones are tracked on the map or, with [cTab](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3438246217), on the tablets- **Dingo CG13** (needs [BWmod](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1200127537)) — the CG13 also jams radios while its IED jammer is switched on
- **CUP DUKE** (needs [CUP Vehicles](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=541888371)) — vehicles with a shown DUKE antenna get a DUKE switch (scroll wheel or ACE) that jams radios and IEDs
- **CUP Drones** (needs [CUP Vehicles](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=541888371)) — the MQ-9 and Pchela-1T can be jammed by drone jammers and show on the Spectrum Device
- **CUP Boomerang** (needs [CUP Vehicles](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=541888371), not Crow's Electronic Warfare) — vehicles with a Boomerang shot finder get shot detection with a hint and map markers
- **RHS USAF DUKE** (needs [RHS USAF](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=843577117)) — vehicles with a DUKE also jam radios while it is online, including reduced range with damaged antennas. The Stryker family gets the DUKE that RHS shows but does not implement
- **RHS USAF Boomerang** (needs [RHS USAF](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=843577117), not Crow's Electronic Warfare) — the M1245 SOCOM vehicles get shot detection with a hint and map markers
- **RHS AFRF R-142N** (needs [RHS AFRF](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=843425103)) — the GAZ-66 (R-142N) jams radios and drones while its mast is deployed
- **RnT Fuchs Hummel** (needs [Redd'n'Tank Vehicles](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1128145626)) — the Fuchs 1A4 Hummel, a jammer vehicle based on the Jäger that jams radios while it is switched on

Radio jamming needs [ACRE](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=751965892). Crow's Electronic Warfare only works in multiplayer.
