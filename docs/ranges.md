# Jammer ranges

Every jammer has an **effective radius** (full strength) and a **falloff radius**, a band *beyond* the effective radius in which the effect fades out. The outer edge is the sum of both.

| Jammer | Effective | Falloff band | Outer edge | Affects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dingo 2 CG13 | 40 m | 60 m | 100 m | ACRE radios |
| DUKE (both antennas) | 50 m | 75 m | 125 m | ACRE radios |
| DUKE (one antenna) | 30 m | 45 m | 75 m | ACRE radios |
| BFT jammer module (default) | 200 m | 400 m | 600 m | cTab BFT markers |

!!! note
    The CG13 and DUKE radii are fixed by the respective mod's IED jammer. The BFT module's radii are editor attributes and can be set freely.
